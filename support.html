🏳️‍🌈 LSN Support at College

The LGBTQ+ Students' Network (LSN) is here to support LGBTQ+ students and allies. 💜

👤 Nathan Cain is the LGBTQ+ Students' Officer.
👤 Jennifer Burns is the LSN Chair.

🤝 Need help, support, or information? Click on a name below to email one of our LSN Representatives:

  • 🌈 Sarah Teasdale: sarah.t6@colc.ac.uk
  • 🌈 Luna Jenkinson: riley.j2@colc.ac.uk
  • 🌈 Jack Howard: jack.h67@colc.ac.uk

💬 LSN Representatives are the first people to contact. They can listen, answer questions, and help you find the right support.

📮 You can also use an LSN postbox to share feedback, ask questions, or raise concerns anonymously.

🏫 For additional support, you can contact Beverley Abbott (SU Coordinator) or the Students' Union team.

💖 Everyone is welcome in our LGBTQ+ Students' Network.
