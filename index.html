📋 Community Guidelines

🤝 Respect everyone's identity, name and pronouns.

❤️ Be kind, welcoming and supportive.

🔒 Keep personal information private.

✅ Ask before sharing information about someone else.

🌈 Respect different experiences within the LGBTQ+ community.

🏫 Follow College and Students' Union rules.

🚫 No bullying, harassment, discrimination or hate.
